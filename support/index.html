01Contact us

Email exth331@gmail.com. To help us answer faster, include your iPhone model, iOS version and a short description of the problem. You can also write from the app: Profile > Help & Support.

02Jmix Premium

  • Manage or cancel: iOS Settings > your name > Subscriptions, or Profile > Manage subscription in the app. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends.
  • Restore: reinstalled the app or got a new iPhone? Open the Premium screen and tap Restore Purchases.
  • One account: Premium belongs to the Jmix account where it was bought. Sign in to that account to use it.
  • Refunds: payments are processed by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

03Account

  • No verification email? Check your Spam folder, then tap Resend in the app.
  • Forgot password? Tap Forgot password on the sign in screen and follow the link in the email.
  • Delete account: Profile > Delete account. Your data is permanently removed. Deleting the account does not cancel Premium.

04AI ideas and plans

Jmix uses AI only after you allow it. You can turn it off anytime in Profile > AI personalization. AI suggestions are for inspiration and are not professional advice.
