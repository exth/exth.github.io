Privacy Policy

What information the Jmix app collects, how it is used and shared, and how you can delete it.

Effective: November 13, 2026 Last updated: November 13, 2026

01Who we are

Jmix is an iPhone app that suggests career paths based on your typical day, builds a step-by-step plan with AI and helps you work toward it every day.

Jmix is developed by Vadim Shut, who is responsible for your data. Contact: exth331@gmail.com.

02Data we collect

  • Account: email address, and your name if you sign in with Apple or Google. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and we never see them.
  • Your content: the role you want and the description of your typical day, the goals you add, your progress and daily check-ins.
  • Generated content: career ideas and goal plans created for you by AI.
  • Usage data: app events, device model, iOS and app version, and approximate country, collected by Firebase Analytics, plus records of AI requests (time and type, without your texts) used for daily limits.
  • Advertising data (free version): device and connection data used by Google AdMob, and the Apple Advertising Identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking.
  • Purchases: payments are processed by Apple. We only receive your subscription status, never card details.

Jmix does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.

03How we use data

  • To create your account and sign you in.
  • To generate career ideas and goal plans.
  • To save and sync your goals and progress.
  • To apply usage limits and prevent abuse.
  • To understand how the app is used and improve it.
  • To show ads in the free version and provide Premium.

We do not sell your personal data.

04AI features

Jmix uses Google Gemini, a third-party AI service, through Google Cloud Vertex AI.

i

Nothing is sent to AI without your permission. Before the first request, Jmix shows what will be sent and asks you to allow it. You can withdraw permission at any time in the app settings.

  • What is sent: your desired role, your typical day text and the chosen career.
  • What is not sent: your name, email, account ID or device identifiers.
  • Google uses this data only to return a response and does not use it to train AI models.

Please do not include sensitive personal information in your texts. AI suggestions may be inaccurate and are not professional advice.

05Ads and tracking

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Premium has no ads.

  • iOS asks for your permission before any tracking. You can change it in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • In the EEA, UK and some US states, you can manage ad consent or opt out of personalized ads in Jmix Settings > Privacy choices.

07Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. AI request records are kept up to 12 months.

You can delete your account at any time in Jmix > Profile > Delete account. Your account and all related data are permanently deleted from our servers within 30 days. Deleting the app alone does not delete your account. To cancel Premium, use iOS Settings > your name > Subscriptions.

You can also request deletion by email at exth331@gmail.com.

08Your rights

Depending on where you live (including the EEA, UK and California), you can access, correct, delete or get a copy of your data, object to or restrict its use, opt out of personalized ads and withdraw consent at any time. EEA users may also complain to their data protection authority.

To use these rights, email exth331@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days.

09Children

Jmix is not intended for children under 13 (under 16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

10Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will change the date above. Important changes will be announced in the app.

Questions? Email exth331@gmail.com.
